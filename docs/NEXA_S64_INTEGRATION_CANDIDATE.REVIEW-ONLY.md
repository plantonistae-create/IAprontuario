## A. Export trust boundary
The S6.3 worker both acquires and commits delivery; SQL trusts its claim of signature validation. A compromised acquisition login can directly invoke commit_delivery. S6.4 overlay for a FRESH disposable nexa_s63_lab revokes commit/ingest from acquisition roles and introduces per-worker independent verifier login mapping. Verifier cannot acquire. The verifier validates signed proof, builds the artifact itself, calls the existing locked decision path and rechecks ingestion. Database additionally rejects nonfinite or excessively long proof expiry. The overlay does not verify Ed25519 in PostgreSQL; a compromised verifier remains in the trusted computing base.

Production design: independent acquisition, signing and release processes with scoped credentials; signer receives canonical snapshot from authenticated source, never signs arbitrary client payload; release verifier pins issuer/key, checks proof, derives artifact itself, owns ONLY its restricted commit/ingest credential; no shared process memory, token, role membership or secret with acquisition. Restrict transport and logs, no raw material exposed to client. Independent authorization/audit of key provisioning, revoke, rotate, and monitor. The current focal lab test has separate SCRAM sessions but a common test harness process. Actual independent process isolation and signer source authentication remain NOT_RUN. Do not represent a database privilege check as cryptographic validation.

## B. Dataset family partition
Registry (trusted bootstrap, immutable mapping dataset -> family UUID) and family_groups(family, patient_group, purpose) make the split apply across dataset versions, names, encounters and owners in the same family. Serialize releases on family + protected patient group, keep assignment immutable, reject unregistered datasets at delivery. Family definition must be approved by data governance across tasks and releases; no arbitrary user-chosen family/reset. Patient group in the lab is a synthetic UUID supplied by bootstrap. Real cross-owner identity linkage requires an approved privacy-preserving authority and evaluation leakage policy; never infer identity from clinical notes or expose grouping keys. Before integrating with existing inventory, reconcile prior splits and block unknown/conflicting mappings; overlay intentionally aborts on nonempty legacy delivery/group tables.

## C. Clinical audio is not training audio
Keep M3, the encrypted local vault and remote preservation queue unchanged. For future owner pilot:
1. Capture original encoded bytes and preserve owner/encounter/session/sourceVersion, MIME/codec, ordered sequence, size and digest. Mark interrupted sessions partial; never describe partial audio as complete. Do not transcode the only original or change bitrate without quality tests.
2. Finalization only after exact chunk coverage and manifest; remote protection only after decrypted readback matches original local bytes and manifest scope/digest. Keep local until separate authorized retention policy AND current remote proof. Retention stays OFF. A second device verifies remote data before use; policy projection here conservatively requires local evidence for declaring a newly backed-up session remote_verified, not a replacement for remote recovery.
3. Use encrypted storage, independent envelope keys, owner-scoped access and clinical_backup grants for preservation. Audit recovery, outages and custody without content in logs. KMS continuity, MFA, regional assessment, storage/quota, device testing, consent/clinical-use basis and ability to recover after device loss remain pilot prerequisites. No production activation in S6.4.
4. Future ASR dataset is a SEPARATE controlled derived asset: permission by task=ASR, purpose, modality=audio, session/manifest/version, source rights and transcript review. Audio voice is identifying; no claim of anonymization via metadata stripping, pseudonym or regex. Require approved handling of voice, background speakers, names, consent and applicable terms; isolate processing and review. Prefer licensed or independently recorded synthetic audio until authorized. Derive segments with timestamps and independent human reference text, record transformation version and hashes of derivative/audio-transcript pair; do not export original backup objects, keys or internal IDs. Apply family split and rights recheck at release and ingestion. Current candidate ASR remains text-only, no audio training implemented.
5. Track preservation readiness independently of dataset eligibility. No grant for assistance/clinical_backup may authorize training, evaluation or purge. No output from third-party AI becomes a training label automatically or via physician approval alone; verify source terms and lawful rights independently.
